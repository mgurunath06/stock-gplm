An architectural rule library of **300 non-fundamental, algorithmic trading and screening strategies** tailored for Indian equities (NSE/BSE) can be structured across **six distinct quantitative pillars**, featuring **50 concrete, rule-based strategies in each category** (`6 × 50 = 300 strategies`). Every strategy includes its market psychology ("What it means") and exact mathematical/logical pseudo-code so you can ingest them directly into your app's rule engine.

### The 6 Non-Fundamental Strategy Pillars (Taxonomy Discovery)

1. **Trend Following & Momentum (50 Strategies):** Moving average systems, directional trend indicators, breakouts, and relative strength.
2. **Volume, Delivery & Accumulation (50 Strategies):** Volume-Price Analysis (VPA), NSE/BSE delivery spikes, institutional footprint, and volume oscillators.
3. **Mean Reversion, Oscillators & Divergence (50 Strategies):** Overbought/oversold stretches, momentum divergences, statistical Z-score reversions, and indicator hooks.
4. **Volatility, Range Contraction & Intraday Gaps (50 Strategies):** Volatility squeezes, ATR expansions, Opening Range Breakouts (ORB), and gap mechanics.
5. **Price Action, Candlesticks & Market Structure (50 Strategies):** Multi-candle patterns, Smart Money Concepts (SMC/ICT), Fibonacci retracements, and geometric chart structures.
6. **Indian F&O, Quantitative & Relative Strength (50 Strategies):** Open Interest (OI) build-ups, PCR/Max Pain mechanics, sector rotation vs. Nifty 50, and calendar/seasonality anomalies.

12. **Futures Premium Expansion (Basis Surge):** Futures price trading at a widening premium over Spot price (Cost of Carry expansion), indicating bullish demand. `Basis = (Futures_Close - Spot_Close) / Spot_Close; Basis > 0.008 AND Basis[t] > Basis[t-1]`
13. **Futures Discount Backwardation Squeeze:** Stock Futures deeply in discount (`Futures < Spot`) due to heavy retail shorting, breaking above spot VWAP (short squeeze setup). `Futures_Close < Spot_Close AND Spot_Price breaks 5-day High`
14. **Rollover Percentage + Roll Cost Surge (Expiry Week):** During NSE monthly expiry week, high rollover % combined with rising roll cost proves bulls are carrying positions to next series. `Rollover_Pct > 78% (or > 3M Avg) AND Roll_Cost_Bps expanding AND Price > VWAP`
15. **Put-Call Ratio (PCR) Extreme Oversold Reversal:** Stock or Index OI PCR dropping to extreme bearish levels where puts are over-owned. `PCR_OI < 0.60 AND PCR_OI turns upward AND Price > Prev_Hour_High`
16. **PCR Bullish Trend Support (Put Writing Dominance):** Heavy Put writing raising PCR above 1.2 during an uptrend. `PCR_OI crosses above 1.15 AND Spot_Close > EMA(20)`
17. **Option Chain Call Wall Breakout (Gamma Squeeze):** When Spot price clears the strike price with the highest Call Open Interest, Call writers scramble to cover, creating an explosive gamma rally. `Spot_Price > Strike(MAX_Call_OI) AND Call_OI at that strike starts declining (negative Change in OI)`
18. **Option Chain Put Wall Floor Bounce:** The strike with maximum Put Open Interest acts as a hard institutional floor. `Spot_Low tests Strike(MAX_Put_OI) AND Change_in_Put_OI > 0 (writers defending strike)`
19. **Max Pain Convergence (Expiry Week Mean Reversion):** During the final 2–3 days before NSE monthly expiry, stock price gravitates toward the Option Max Pain strike. `Days_to_Expiry <= 3 AND abs(Spot - Max_Pain) / Spot > 3% AND Price moving toward Max_Pain`
20. **Implied Volatility (IV) Rank Crush Post-Event:** Selling volatility or entering directional swings once IV Rank drops from > 80 after earnings/budget. `IV_Rank(252)[t-1] > 80 AND IV_Rank[t] drops > 15 points AND Price breaks event high`
21. **India VIX Spike > 20 Panic Washout Buy:** When India VIX surges > 20% in a week and starts mean-reverting lower, high-beta Nifty stocks stage sharp rallies. `India_VIX > 20 AND India_VIX[t] < India_VIX[t-1] AND Stock_RSI(14) > 35`
22. **F&O Ban List Exit Momentum:** Stocks in the NSE F&O Ban list (Market-Wide Position Limit > 95%) that drop below 80% MWPL exit the ban and see fresh institutional derivative inflows. `Status[t-1] == "In_Ban" AND Status[t] == "Out_of_Ban" AND Price > High[t-1]`
23. **Statistical Pairs Trading (Cointegrated Spread Z-Score):** Trading two historically linked Indian stocks (e.g., HDFC Bank vs ICICI Bank, TCS vs Infosys) when their spread diverges. `ADF_P_Value(StockA, StockB) < 0.05 AND Spread_Z_Score < -2.0` (Long Stock A, Short Stock B)
24. **Stock vs. Sector ETF Mean Reversion:** A major constituent diverges > 2 SDs below its parent sector index (e.g., SBI vs Nifty Bank) while trend remains intact. `Z_Score(Stock_Close / Sector_Close, 20) < -2.0 AND Stock_Close > SMA(200)`
25. **Kalman Filter Dynamic Mean Tracking:** State-space estimation tracking the true underlying equilibrium price without fixed lookback lag. `Close crosses above Kalman_Estimate AND Kalman_Velocity > 0`
26. **Hurst Exponent Regime Router (H > 0.5 vs H < 0.5):** Mathematical meta-filter that detects if a stock is currently trending (`H > 0.55`) or mean-reverting (`H < 0.45`). `Hurst(100) > 0.60 -> Enable Trend Rules; Hurst(100) < 0.40 -> Enable Mean Reversion Rules`
27. **Shannon Entropy Predictability Filter:** Measuring randomness in binary up/down day sequences; low entropy signals structured institutional order flow. `Sample_Entropy(Close_Returns, 20) in bottom 20th percentile AND ROC(10) > 0`
28. **Autocorrelation (Serial Correlation) Momentum:** Positive lag-1 to lag-5 return autocorrelation indicating persistent multi-day institutional execution. `Autocorr(Returns, Lag=1, Window=30) > 0.20 AND Return[t] > 0`
29. **Variance Ratio Test Breakout:** Variance of 10-day returns exceeding 10x the variance of 1-day returns proves a non-random trending regime. `Var(Return_10D) / (10 * Var(Return_1D)) > 1.30 AND Close > SMA(20)`
30. **Fama-French 12-1 Momentum (Skipping Recent Month):** Classic quantitative factor: ranking stocks by 12-month return excluding the most recent 1 month (which tends to mean-revert). `Return(t-252 to t-21) in top 10% of universe AND Return(t-21 to t) < 0 (dip buy)`
31. **52-Week High Anchoring Factor (George & Hwang):** Stocks trading closest to their 52-week high (`Close / High_52W > 0.95`) have the strongest post-news drift due to psychological anchoring. `Close / MAX(High, 252) > 0.95 AND Volume > SMA(Volume, 20)`
32. **Amihud Illiquidity Ratio Drop (Liquidity Awakening):** A sharp drop in price impact per rupee traded shows deep institutional liquidity entering a mid-cap stock. `Amihud_Ratio(20) = Avg(abs(Return) / Traded_Value); Trigger when Amihud drops 50% below 100D avg`
33. **Turn-of-the-Month (SIP Inflow) Effect:** In India, massive retail SIP inflows hit mutual funds between the 28th of Month 1 and the 5th of Month 2, lifting Nifty/Midcap leaders. `Calendar_Day >= 28 OR Calendar_Day <= 4 AND Stock in Nifty_Midcap_100 AND Close > EMA(20)`
34. **Pre-Expiry Thursday/Wednesday Short Squeeze:** Short option writers covering positions on the day before NSE weekly/monthly expiry. `Day == Expiry_Minus_1 AND Spot > Yesterday_High AND Call_OI unwinding at ATM strikes`
35. **Post-Earnings Announcement Drift (PEAD Technical Proxy):** A stock that gaps up > 4% on quarterly results volume continues drifting higher for 20–40 trading days. `Gap_Up > 4% on Volume > 3x Avg within last 10 days AND Price pulls back to 10-EMA`
36. **Overnight vs. Intraday Return Divergence:** Stocks where cumulative overnight returns (`Open[t] - Close[t-1]`) are strongly positive while intraday returns are flat show institutional overnight accumulation. `SUM(Overnight_Return, 20) > 8% AND SUM(Intraday_Return, 20) < 0`
37. **Up-Minus-Down (UMD) Volatility Asymmetry:** Upside semi-deviation (volatility on green days) vastly exceeding downside semi-deviation (volatility on red days). `StdDev(Positive_Returns, 30) / StdDev(Negative_Returns, 30) > 1.6`
38. **Skewness Breakout (Positive Return Skew):** Distribution of 60-day daily returns shifting to high positive skewness (frequent small dips, explosive green days). `Skewness(Daily_Returns, 60) > 1.0 AND Close > SMA(50)`
39. **Kaufman Efficiency Ratio (ER) Smooth Trend:** Net price displacement divided by total sum of absolute daily moves approaching 1.0 (zero noise). `abs(Close[t] - Close[t-10]) / SUM(abs(Close[i] - Close[i-1]), 10) > 0.65`
40. **Dynamic Time Warping (DTW) Pattern Match:** Algorithmic template matching comparing the last 20 normalized bars against historical pre-breakout signatures. `DTW_Distance(Normalized_Close[t-20..t], Ideal_VCP_Template) < Threshold`
41. **Z-Score of Volume-Weighted Price Velocity:** Combining price Z-score and volume Z-score into a single composite institutional ignition score. `Z_Score(ROC_5, 60) * Z_Score(Volume, 60) > 4.0 AND ROC_5 > 0`
42. **Beta-Adjusted Residual Momentum:** Stripping out Nifty 50 market movement (`Return_Stock - Beta * Return_Nifty`) to isolate pure idiosyncratic stock momentum. `SUM(Idiosyncratic_Return, 20) in top 5% of NSE universe`
43. **Maximum Adverse Excursion (MAE) Tight Trend:** Stock advancing for 15+ sessions where no single intraday low breaches the prior 3-day low. `For i in 1..15: Low[t-i] >= MIN(Low, 3)[t-i-1]`
44. **Intraday First-Hour vs. Last-Hour Smart Money Index (SMI):** Retail trades the erratic first 30 mins (`9:15–9:45`); institutions dominate the final hour (`14:30–15:30`). `SMI = Prev_SMI - Return(9:15-9:45) + Return(14:30-15:30); Trigger when SMI hits 20-day high`
45. **FII / DII Cash Flow Regime Alignment:** Conditioning high-beta breakout strategies to fire only when net institutional cash flow (`FII_Net + DII_Net`) is positive over 5 days. `SUM(FII_Cash_Net + DII_Cash_Net, 5) > 0 AND Nifty_50 > EMA(20)`
46. **52-Week Low to High Recovery Velocity (Turnaround Quant):** Stocks that were down > 40% from highs, formed a 3-month flat bottom, and just reclaimed their 200-DMA. `Max_Drawdown_1Yr > 40% AND Range(60) < 15% AND Close crosses above SMA(200)`
47. **Cross-Asset Ratio Signal (Nifty Bank / Nifty 50 Ratio):** Using BankNifty relative strength as a macro risk-on filter for domestic cyclical stocks. `SMA(BankNifty / Nifty50, 10) > SMA(BankNifty / Nifty50, 30)`
48. **Dual Momentum (Absolute + Relative):** Antonacci framework: stock must beat both its own 6-month past (Absolute Momentum > Risk-Free Rate) AND beat the Nifty 50 (Relative Momentum). `ROC(126) > 3.5% (6M T-Bill proxy) AND ROC(126) > Nifty_ROC(126)`
49. **Sortino Momentum Ranking:** Ranking momentum stocks by dividing 6-month return purely by downside deviation, avoiding erratic pump-and-dump stocks. `Sortino_6M = Return(126) / Downside_StdDev(126) > 2.5`
50. **Multi-Factor Quant Composite Score (Trend + Volume + RS + Vol-Compression):** Master ingestion rule combining the 4 non-fundamental pillars into a 0–100 composite rank. `0.25*Rank(RS_6M) + 0.25*Rank(Delivery_Spike) + 0.25*Rank(ADX_Trend) + 0.25*Rank(1/BB_Width) > 85`

### How to Architect This in Your App's Rule Engine

Instead of hardcoding 300 separate scripts, build an **AST (Abstract Syntax Tree) or JSON Expression Evaluator** around **5 atomic primitives**:

* **Data Series (`Series`):** `Open, High, Low, Close, Volume, Delivery_Pct, OI, Futures_Close, Nifty_Close`
* **Transforms (`Indicators`):** `SMA, EMA, RSI, ATR, BB_Upper, BB_Lower, VWAP, ADX, Supertrend, Z_Score, MAX, MIN, ROC`
* **Operators (`Comparators`):** `>, <, >=, <=, ==, crosses_above, crosses_below, between`
* **Lookbacks (`Offsets`):** `[t]`, `[t-1]`, `[t-n]`, `Window(20)`
* **Regime Tag (`Metadata`):** Tag every strategy with `"regime": "TREND" | "MEAN_REVERSION" | "VOLATILITY_BREAKOUT"` so your app automatically applies the right stop-loss logic (e.g., `2x ATR Trailing Stop` for Trend vs `SMA(5) Exit` for Mean Reversion).
